# CraftLink AI — SIH 2-Minute Judge Demonstration Script (SIH26090)

## Overview for Presenters
- **Target Time**: 1 minute 45 seconds – 2 minutes
- **Core Message**: *"The artisan does not need to learn photography, English copywriting, SEO, or e-commerce pricing. They only TAKE A PHOTO and SPEAK NATURALLY. The AI handles the rest."*

---

## Step-by-Step Live Presentation Flow

### [0:00 – 0:20] The Problem & Hook
> *"Respected Judges, India has over 7 million rural artisans creating world-class handicrafts. Yet over 80% struggle to sell online because of poor product photography, language barriers, complex e-commerce listing forms, and exploitation by middlemen who underprice their work.*
> 
> *Introducing **CraftLink AI** — From Handmade to Market-Ready in Minutes."*

---

### [0:20 – 0:45] Step 1: Computer Vision Transformation
1. Click **"SIH LIVE DEMO"** or upload a messy photo (e.g., Banarasi Silk Saree or Blue Pottery vase photographed on a messy floor).
2. Point to the **Before / After interactive split slider**:
> *"Notice the raw photo on the left — cluttered background and poor lighting. Our AI Computer Vision module runs CLAHE contrast balancing, segments the craft from the cluttered background, and creates a studio-quality catalog image with realistic grounding shadows."*

---

### [0:45 – 1:10] Step 2 & 3: Speech AI & Zero-Hallucination Extraction
1. Tap the **Microphone** or select a sample Hindi voice prompt:
   *"यह शुद्ध बनारसी कतान सिल्क साड़ी है। इसमें असली सोने की जरी का काम है और 6 दिन का समय लगा है।"*
2. Show the real-time transcript & language identification (`Hindi`).
3. Show the **Extracted Structured Metadata card**:
> *"Our NLP engine extracts the exact craft type, material, dimensions, and production time. Notice our Responsible AI policy: because the artisan did not state the weight, our system marks it as 'Needs Confirmation' rather than hallucinating fake data."*

---

### [1:10 – 1:35] Step 4 & 5: Multilingual Listings & Fair-Trade Pricing
1. Toggle between **English and Hindi listings**:
> *"The AI generates high-converting marketplace titles, storytelling descriptions, technical specifications, and SEO keywords in multiple Indian languages."*
2. Scroll to the **Smart Pricing Card**:
> *"Instead of arbitrary prices, our engine uses real economics: Material Cost (₹2,200) + Skilled Labor for 6 days (₹4,800) + Packaging (₹250) + a Random Forest ML model trained on regional guild benchmarks. It recommends an optimal suggested price of ₹9,850 with a transparent 'Why this price?' breakdown to protect the artisan from exploitation."*

---

### [1:35 – 2:00] Step 6: Confirmation, Marketplace & Export
1. Click **"Confirm & Publish to Catalog"**.
2. Switch to the **Buyer Marketplace** tab to show the live listing.
3. Switch to the **Admin & Impact Analytics** tab and click **"Export CSV"** and **"Export JSON"**.
> *"With one click, the product is published to the catalog and ready for direct buyer inquiries or integration into ONDC and government portals via standard CSV and JSON feeds.*
> 
> *Thank you! We welcome your questions."*
