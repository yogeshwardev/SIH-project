# CraftLink AI — Enterprise E-Commerce Platform Presentation Walkthrough

## Overview for Presenters
- **Target Time**: 2 minutes
- **Core Message**: *"The artisan does not need to learn studio photography, English copywriting, SEO, or complex e-commerce pricing algorithms. They only TAKE A PHOTO and SPEAK NATURALLY in their native language. The AI pipeline handles the rest."*

---

## Step-by-Step Platform Presentation Flow

### [0:00 – 0:20] The Opportunity & Direct Market Linkage
> *"India has over 7 million master artisans creating world-class GI-certified handicrafts. Yet over 80% struggle to sell on digital marketplaces because of poor product photography, language barriers, complex e-commerce listing forms, and intermediary exploitation.*
> 
> *Introducing **CraftLink AI** — Direct Artisan E-Commerce, 0% Middlemen Commission, and Automated AI Studio Cataloging."*

---

### [0:20 – 0:45] Step 1: Computer Vision Studio Transformation
1. Select a craft catalog template or upload a raw photo (e.g., Banarasi Silk Saree or Blue Pottery vase photographed in a workshop).
2. Point to the **Before / After interactive split slider**:
> *"Notice the raw photo on the left — cluttered background and poor lighting. Our AI Computer Vision module runs CLAHE contrast balancing, segments the craft from the cluttered background, and creates a studio-quality catalog image with realistic grounding shadows."*

---

### [0:45 – 1:10] Step 2 & 3: Speech AI & Zero-Hallucination Extraction
1. Tap the mic button and speak in Hindi or English (or click the catalog preset audio).
2. Point to the **live bilingual transcription**:
> *"The artisan speaks in Hindi or English. Our speech recognition pipeline transcribes the audio, and our NLP entity extractor extracts structured attributes without hallucinations: craft type, fabric/material, dimensions, and craft origin."*

---

### [1:10 – 1:35] Step 3 & 4: Multilingual Copywriting & Fair-Trade Pricing
1. Point to the generated English & Hindi product descriptions:
> *"The platform generates SEO-optimized bilingual listings in real time.*
> 
> *Next, our hybrid pricing engine calculates a fair-trade recommended price using regional cost economics and machine learning benchmarks to guarantee sustainable wages."*

---

### [1:35 – 2:00] Step 5: Enterprise Admin Governance & Consumer Storefront
1. Switch to the **Admin Governance Portal** to show the real-time approval queue:
> *"All products undergo automated GI authenticity scoring and admin compliance checks before instant publication to the direct consumer marketplace."*
2. Switch back to the consumer store to demonstrate seamless direct purchasing.
